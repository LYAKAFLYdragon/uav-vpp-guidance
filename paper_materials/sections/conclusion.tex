\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

We present a hierarchical guidance architecture that combines an optional virtual pursuit point (VPP) generator with a line-of-sight-rate (LOS-rate) guidance law, trained and tuned via a bilevel PPO and CEM-EMA framework. A controlled 5-seed fair comparison on the JSBSim F-16 high-fidelity backend (1440 total evaluation episodes) yields four conclusions.

First, the \textbf{VPP architecture is the only method achieving non-zero success on the disadvantage engagement geometry} ($20\%$ [12,28] vs $0\%$ for both No-VPP and E2E). The VPP policy's 2987~m mean offset on disadvantage---$3.2\times$ larger than on other scenarios---evidences a learned safety-valve strategy that reduces crash rate from $100\%$ (No-VPP) to $20\%$ (Fisher $p = 6.5 \times 10^{-37}$), trading timeout for crash avoidance in accordance with the reward structure.

Second, the \textbf{hierarchical design strictly dominates end-to-end DRL} on the challenging ($100\%$ vs $75\%$, $p = 4.8 \times 10^{-10}$) and neutral ($100\%$ vs $87.5\%$, $p = 5.0 \times 10^{-5}$) scenarios. The E2E policy's persistent $12.5\%$ crash rate across both scenarios, even after $2$~M training steps, indicates a structural inability to internalize flight-envelope constraints that the LOS-rate guidance law provides by construction.

Third, \textbf{CEM-EMA gain optimization is a major but architecture-conditional performance driver}. It improves success rate by $+14.47$~pp on the simple backend, but without the VPP safety margin, even optimally-tuned gains cannot prevent $100\%$ crash on disadvantage under JSBSim aerodynamics. Gains and offset are complementary: gains drive nominal performance; offset provides safety margin.

Fourth, the \textbf{VPP offset is not a universal performance booster}---it provides no measurable advantage over the zero-offset variant in three of four scenarios on both the simple and JSBSim backends. Its contribution is specifically a safety retrofit for the worst-case geometry, consistent with the architecture's design intent of adding learned degrees of freedom only where the classical guidance law is insufficient.

Future work will focus on three directions: (1) converting the VPP disadvantage timeout ($60\%$) into success through curriculum learning that progressively tightens the range envelope without reintroducing crash risk, (2) extending to 10+ training seeds to resolve the seed-level variance on disadvantage and confirm the episode-level Fisher result at the seed level, and (3) investigating whether the seed~0 policy's successful disadvantage strategy (100\% SR) can be distilled or reproduced through targeted initialization or behavioral cloning from the high-performing seed.
