This section reports the evidence currently available in the repository and separates it from the experiments that must still be run before final submission. The distinction is intentional. The paper's contribution is an auditable comparison protocol, so results that do not satisfy the frozen protocol are not treated as conclusive.

\subsection{Auditable fair-comparison protocol}\label{sec:fair_protocol}

The final comparison protocol is defined by the following reproducible clauses.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Observation space:} all learned methods use the same 16-dimensional normalized observation vector produced by \texttt{src/uav\_vpp\_guidance/envs/observation.py}; VPP-specific diagnostic variables are logged but not added unless explicitly stated as an ablation.
    \item \textbf{Training scaffold:} PPO is the shared optimizer. The target full-budget matrix uses the same MLP scale and reward definition for hierarchical VPP, zero-offset No-VPP, and the end-to-end RL baseline. Existing repository configurations are currently at 200k--300k PPO steps; the requested final matrix extends E2E to 2M steps and fixes VPP/No-VPP budgets before claims are promoted from exploratory to main-table status.
    \item \textbf{Random seeds:} the main table is frozen at 10 independent training seeds when checkpoints are available. The current repository contains 30 evaluation seeds on the simple backend and 10 evaluation seeds on a corrected JSBSim regression run, but it does not yet contain a completed 10-training-seed JSBSim VPP/No-VPP/E2E matrix.
    \item \textbf{Simulator backend:} simple point-mass results are used for mechanism isolation; Defence Technology main claims require JSBSim F-16 evaluation.
    \item \textbf{Metrics:} every method is evaluated by success rate, crash rate, timeout/out-of-bounds rate, mean return, standard deviation, 95\% confidence interval, effect size, and an exact binary-outcome test when applicable.
\end{enumerate}

This protocol follows the reporting concerns raised for deep-RL experiments by Henderson et al.~\cite{henderson2018deep} and Patterson et al.~\cite{patterson2023empirical}. Results below are labeled according to whether they satisfy the protocol.

\subsection{External baselines and robustness experiments}\label{sec:external_and_robustness}

The repository evidence already shows why external guidance baselines cannot be omitted: pure PN without VPP succeeds in selected high-energy tail-chase candidates where several VPP-based variants fail. The new \texttt{paper\_materials/scripts/run\_external\_baselines.py} evaluates pure pursuit, pure PN, and APN under the same scenario and metric schema. The new \texttt{paper\_materials/scripts/run\_robustness.py} evaluates wind, observation noise, actuator delay, and guidance-parameter sensitivity. Until these runs are executed with paper-safe checkpoints, the manuscript cannot claim that the bottleneck is exclusively in the predictor, the guidance law, or the physics of the geometry.
